**programmable logic devices:**
many ways to implement computational circuit:

**SPLDs**:
* low gate count
* predictable timing
* low cost
* mostly used for address decoding
* can be much more power efficient than larger ones
* timing can be more precise on these smaller chips with much smaller interconnect
* has read-only memory (ROM)
* has n inputs and m outputs with ROM that has 2^n x m bits

**CPLDs**
* low to medium gates 500 to 12k
* predictable timing
* low to medium cost

FPGA:
* medium to high gate count - 3k to 5M
* unpredictable timing
* medium to high cost
* standard off the shelf prodicts
* programmable by user several times
* large chip size 
* slower than MPGAs
